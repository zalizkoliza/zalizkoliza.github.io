Book covers

2023 – 2025

Cover and interior illustration for a bedtime story and a hand-drawn recipe book.

The Moon Who Lost Her Light2023 – 2025

A gentle story about the moon who loses her light and sets off on a journey to find it. Along the way, she meets night creatures who help her discover the strength within. Soft, dreamlike illustrations and poetic narration make it perfect for children, and soothing for adults, too. It’s a story about healing, hope, and finding light in unexpected places.

The Witch’s Pantry2024

“The Witch’s Pantry”: a hand-drawn recipe book filled with potions, elixirs, and edible spells. Each page blends cozy kitchen magic with a touch of folklore and whimsy. Thematic chapters guide readers through charming, enchanted recipes inspired by herbs, nature, and old-world witchcraft. Part cookbook, part spellbook, perfect for anyone who loves a little magic in their meals.
